# Verify housing notices and financing conditions

Use this procedure for a requested source refresh or a material financing assumption. Read the current private analysis and source manifest first. Keep accepted filing copies immutable.

## Official notices and corrections

1. Search the actual SH public notice UI for the declared programme and date range. Include separate names such as long-term lease and Mirinae. Preserve the filters, total count and every results page.
2. Wait for the result rows to load before capturing them. Reconcile the collected row count with the visible total; a loaded table shell can temporarily have no rows.
3. Separate recruitment notices, corrected recruitment terms, applicant/result notices and contract/move-in instructions. Read the attached current document and any substantive correction. A corrected contract date does not automatically alter deposits or eligibility.
4. Download the official attachment and compare its SHA-256 with the analyzed copy. If the bytes differ, compare full text, relevant tables, images, dates and operative terms before changing calculations. Record the exact confirmed notice set; do not promote a sample to all historical rounds.
5. Keep the earlier source, extracted rows and accepted attachment. Record official verification in a separate intake and make the generator read it. A manual manifest update that regeneration discards is not a durable correction.

The official public notice UI is [SH notices](https://www.i-sh.co.kr/main/lay2/program/S1T294C297/www/brd/m_247/list.do). Search navigation can move between SH board layouts. The detail URL can be shared across POST-based records; preserve the exact title, public record ID and observed preview link as well as the snapshot. Missing search results do not establish deletion or the absence of all later corrections.

If a current original link reports that its posting period has expired, preserve that message and record identity. An official webzine or a secondary archive can recover a prior public record ID, but a search service's cached official text does not establish current download access, byte equivalence or the absence of later corrections. Do not extend one notice's expiry to other rounds. After bounded checks of the official notice, application notice board and retained public link, request the agency's held final notice and substantive correction files. Keep the request prepared separately from accepted filings until it is authorized and delivered. Record partial pagination explicitly.

## Deferral and bank lending

Confirm these as separate conditions:

| Condition | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Deferral eligibility and timing | SH current programme and contract instructions |
| Upfront payment and refund amount | Contract/deferral instructions, including actual paid versus nominal deposit |
| Deferred interest and annual reset | SH year-specific rate and formula |
| Renewal increase | Whether assessed on full nominal deposit; whether the increase must be paid separately |
| Loan maximum and deposit cap | Exact bank/guarantee product, not another product's ceiling |
| Applicable interest | Dated rate table and its benchmark household; individual approval stays separate |
| Collateral and guarantee | Return-claim assignment/pledge, enforceable amount and bank/guarantor approval |
| Joint use | A statement or agreement covering SH deferral and that bank/guarantee product together |

A public bank maximum and an SH deferral scheme do not independently establish joint approval. Record unavailable confirmation as pending, with the specific missing condition. Do not replace it with a blanket claim that financing is possible or impossible. An informational source check does not authorize sending a bank inquiry or a new petition.

Trace calculator explanations to the actual current lender/guarantor rule. HF's published deposit cap, guarantee amount, payment execution rule and exceptions do not establish SGI approval. Keep the bank loan amount separate from the guarantee amount. A collateral ratio fitting the paid deposit arithmetically is also not an underwriting decision. Prepare questions about the landlord's return-claim handling separately from the lender/guarantor's acceptance, income recognition, cost-ratio treatment and execution procedure.

## Calculations and report wording

- Use the actual selected notice's deposit. Keep household income and seven-year savings fixed when comparing financing paths.
- Compare joint-use and no-joint-use branches if the condition materially changes access. A borrowing requirement above the reviewed product maximum is a funding gap, not an approved loan.
- Separate initial cash, bank interest, deferred interest, repayment, renewal increases and household costs. Contract cash is part of own funds, not another amount to add twice.
- Show current published rate cases when useful. Label a product's lowest rate and its conditions; do not treat it as every borrower's approved rate. A confirmed unchanged rate does not require replacing valid arithmetic.
- Value price support using an explicit market-deposit ratio and the same financing baseline. Do not silently substitute deposit interest for loan interest. Keep deferral principal, annual interest savings, future purchase discount and fiscal expenditure distinct.
- Preserve a benchmark with no family support. Do not use unknown approval to erase the policy-design problem; keep the funding requirement and request for an institutionally verified path clear.

For a consequential revision, independently recompute the affected values and inspect every authored PDF page. Update the current working report/pointer, not the accepted filing manifest. State which numerical conclusions changed and which inputs were confirmed unchanged.
