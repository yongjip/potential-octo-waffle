# Review a policy reply against its evidence

Use this procedure after acquiring a complete new policy reply. Keep case-specific plans, receipts, original questions, replies, disclosure decisions and prepared follow-ups in ignored private storage. This procedure does not authorize filing or contacting another recipient.

## Load the delivered scope and review plan

1. Replay the tracker and match the receipt. Read the accepted original and latest accepted supplement, not only a later draft.
2. Read `filing.reply_review_plan` when present. Its `path` identifies the private comparison plan; `data/complaints/current.json` provides the corresponding navigation pointer.
3. Preserve the complete new reply, displayed date, source URL and SHA-256. Follow the status-monitoring procedure before recording receipt of an answer.
4. Separate questions actually delivered from later questions or evidence that could not be appended. A new local comparison criterion must not become a retroactively delivered question.

## Compare three evidence sets

Create one row per delivered question: request and location, actual reply and location, related disclosure finding, applicable historical precedent, assessment, remaining decision and next action.

- **Accepted questions:** distinguish a legal eligibility rule, the policy reason for choosing a threshold, real access under payment and financing conditions, and an implementation commitment. A general purpose does not answer every one of these questions.
- **Disclosure decisions:** retain the request's institution, period and subject. A decision that joint affordability records are not held does not establish that every original rule-making record is absent, that no institution ever studied the issue, or that the decision is false. Preserve the difference between non-possession, explicit non-performance and denial of access.
- **Historical precedents:** match the programme, population, operative date and original reason. A past price-sensitive eligibility rule can support asking how the same consideration applies today; it does not establish a current entitlement or an identical context. An estimated increase in eligible households is not proof of payment affordability or actual recipient composition.

Classify delivered questions as answered, partial, unanswered or not applicable, with exact reply evidence for answered/partial entries. Record rejection separately from silence. Keep newly prepared questions in the private plan, not in the accepted question set.

## Select the next action

| New reply | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Identifies existing analysis or an actual decision record | Acquire the named record and verify its date, assumptions and fit to the policy in question. Reconcile it with the disclosure scope before alleging a contradiction. |
| Explains a policy judgement without a quantitative study | Assess the stated reason and trade-off. Ask whether the rule serves the purpose under the demonstrated conditions; do not demand a unique scientifically optimal threshold. |
| Accepts a change or names a concrete review | Track the responsible unit, next milestone and eventual operative guidance or notice. An intention is not implementation. |
| Gives only current law, a generic purpose or an unspecified future review | Prepare one focused follow-up with the missing decision, the disclosure result and the matched historical precedent. Keep related pending requests visible to avoid duplicates. |
| Treats policy design as an individual bank or operational decision | Separate household approval from government eligibility and allocation design. Identify the actual policy owner and necessary coordination. |
| Reveals a specific omitted document or record holder | Consider a targeted record request or appropriate review route based on that evidence. Non-possession alone is not proof of concealment or misconduct. |

Review each arriving reply promptly; do not postpone acquisition because a related case is still pending. Consolidate the remaining issue before preparing another petition. Follow-ups, objections, external messages and changes to an existing filing need their own authorization. A documented plan is not authorization to submit automatically.

## Review a repeated-petition notice

Separate a warning about future closure from an actual repeated-petition closure. Record the earlier receipts and notification dates cited by the authority. A notice that two replies were sent does not establish that every later question was answered or that all closure conditions have been met. Compare the accepted bodies, not just their titles or the common policy topic.

Article 23 of the Civil Petitions Treatment Act requires consideration of the petition's nature, substantive similarity and relationship, and circumstances requiring the same answer. Do not recommend cosmetic rewriting, changing recipients or filing each new notice as a way to reset the count. A new notice number, date or calculation alone does not guarantee separate treatment. Identify the genuinely new facts or legal conditions, why they can affect the answer, and the distinct decision or corrective action requested. Preserve the connection to the earlier issue.

Keep three possible routes separate: policy improvement through the existing council case, acquisition of identified existing records, and review of the authority's handling of unanswered questions. An ombudsman or complaints-review request is a proposed independent review, not a guaranteed finding of misconduct or a mechanism to compel the preferred policy. Verify the current route before execution. Monitoring candidates or preparing a differentiated petition does not authorize automatic filing or a new monitoring schedule.

For an individual ombudsman request, verify the current local ordinance's grievance-investigation procedure rather than applying collective audit-request signature requirements. Put the handling issue first: which delivered questions were examined, why an answer omitted them, and whether a repeated-petition notice was general guidance or an actual similarity assessment. If the authority made that assessment, ask how it considered a justified need to clarify unanswered items. Distinguish overlapping questions from genuinely additional requests by comparing the earlier accepted bodies. A section heading's date is not necessarily the verified save or submission date.

Prepare a compact comparison and a separate source appendix. Preserve the authority's explanations that did answer part of a question, and label the applicant's assessment as such. Keep full source text distinct from marked extracts and preserve any later additional reply. An unanswered disclosure request need not delay a self-contained review of already documented handling; do not assume its eventual result. Have factual, legal and receiving-official reviews check the same final draft and render every attachment page before freezing the packet.

## Tracking and completion

Preserve each receipt, accepted body, attachments, historical reply and displayed deadline. Add a local planning event with the private plan pointer and next action. Do not change portal status, `last_checked_on`, delivered questions or filing acceptance merely because a plan was updated.

Keep the existing query frequency. A due date or planned milestone does not create a new schedule. The existing weekly monitor and user-requested checks after portal notifications can acquire the reply and run this comparison. Notify only meaningful findings and the recommended next action.

Complete the review when the full reply is preserved, each delivered question is assessed, the disclosure/precedent comparison is recorded, and the next action has an owner and an observable completion condition. Keep acceptance, substantive answer, policy decision and actual implementation separate.

## Recorded lessons

When a pending case no longer exposes an edit or additional-material control, preserve the current UI evidence and the undelivered supplement. Do not silently create a replacement petition. Distinguish that route limitation from a merits decision by the agency. Use newly obtained decisions as bounded evidence in a subsequent review, without diluting a demonstrated access barrier or inventing recipient statistics.
