# Plan a traceable policy improvement request

Turn a selected policy request into a short official memo, a tracked written decision and an implementation check. Keep personal drafts, call evidence and actual receipt numbers in ignored complaint storage. A plan is not evidence that a filing or message was sent.

## Fix the request and answer items

State the selected change first and one acceptable fallback. Explain the connected mechanism before adding calculations. For a deposit and income-ceiling issue, connect earned savings, upfront funding, later interest and the admission ceiling. Separate current income from accumulated wealth. Do not add parental-wealth, gift-history or minimum-income tests without a selected policy change.

When the user requests a problem-first opening, precede the selected change with a short conditional explanation of the mismatch. Connect it immediately to the verified programme purpose and the requested correction. A deposit-funded asset amount can conflict with an asset ceiling only under the applicable valuation, deductions, exceptions and timing. Keep those limits beside the detailed evidence. A concise explanation before a second request can clarify its role without adding more answer items. See the [upper-saving-rate and opening lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-upper-saving-rates-and-problem-first-opening-on-2026-10-10).

Write the selected scope affirmatively: identify the criterion to change and the criteria to retain. Avoid an exclusion list of unselected alternatives or a generic guarantee caveat when the actual request and calculation assumptions already define the scope. Keep internal safeguards in the operating record; retain reader-relevant assumptions beside the evidence. See the [scope wording lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-council-scope-wording-on-2026-10-09).

When the user asks to update documents after expanding the selected policy scope, revise the affirmative scope throughout the current memo, body, dossier, policy-focus record and execution pointers. Preserve previous envelopes and attachments. An asset-adjustment proposal cannot coexist with a current blanket instruction to retain the old asset cap. Keep any unselected numeric threshold or formula explicit, and give the institution separate decisions on income abolition, its fallback and deposit-linked asset adjustment within the same first answer item. Lead with verified programme purpose and the funding-path mechanism in a constructive tone. Do not convert an earned-income benchmark into a new origin or income-floor test. See the [joint-proposal revision lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-the-joint-proposal-document-revision-on-2026-10-09).

Track delivery per request component when an existing accepted case covers only the earlier demand. A newly prepared asset request is not a missing answer in a case where it has never been delivered. Preserve actual submitted bodies and ledger records. Update the future reply matrix without rewriting historical acceptance, and mark an older live form for replacement rather than claiming it contains the latest revision.

When a user anticipates an objection to public support for higher-income households, verify the programme's stated target and dated statements before relying on political language. Start with purpose, current housing wealth and the compatibility of the deposit with admission criteria. A stated middle-class target supports reviewing the rule; it does not establish support for an unlimited income ceiling. Keep social mixing's actual meaning separate from eligibility: eliminating discrimination between rental and sale residents does not itself authorise higher-income admission. A different childbirth policy's removal of an income test is an analogy, not a transferable entitlement. Acknowledge scarce-unit allocation and public cost when comparing alternatives; paying a deposit does not eliminate the programme's public subsidy. Keep source-backed draft wording separate from an adopted packet revision. See the [public-purpose framing lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-public-purpose-and-support-objection-framing-on-2026-10-09).

When the user requests a document grounded in public speeches or interviews, produce a shared source-based revision. A biography or reader map is supporting research, not the final deliverable. Connect verified programme purpose, a directly relevant public issue, the matched evidence, the selected change and the requested written decisions. Keep the same proposal for all readers.

Assign each cited passage a limited role: programme purpose, observed issue, evaluation method or material countercontext. Distinguish a funding question from endorsement of an eligibility amendment. Preserve alternative remedies and the speaker's qualifications. A public policy interview can inform a proposed evaluation method without establishing that a particular ordinance automatically governs the programme. Use short, dated passages and source links; keep less relevant examples in research notes.

Address allocation and public cost through a comparison request when the effect is unknown. Do not claim that expanding eligibility costs nothing, leaves competition unchanged or solves every financing problem. Keep a prepared revision candidate separate from the current packet until the document revision is applied consistently across the body, attachment and execution pointers. Validate the portal's actual character limit before describing a body as ready for use. See the [statement-based revision lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-source-based-proposal-strategy-on-2026-10-09).

Use the [official review roleplay](official-roleplay.md) when requested to test tone, objections or a dialogue before transmission. Freeze the input, distinguish a rationale from a full proposal, and evaluate polite phrasing separately from answer completeness. Rotate constructed case-officer, team-lead, legal/budget and council profiles without claiming independent reviewers or predicted official acceptance. Preserve the selected scope and real ledger; hypothetical replies and commitments remain practice records.

When a later document-update request adopts a conditional asset-exit argument, revise the memo, portal body and dossier together. Distinguish initial entry from renewal and place the current childbirth exemption beside the conditional mechanism. A higher income is not a measured shorter support duration. Preserve the selected demands and answer count rather than adding a new withdrawal rule. Version the envelope and attachment after complete rendering, synchronise current nested pointers, and retain historical packets and actual receipts. Follow the [entry/renewal revision lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-the-entry-and-renewal-document-revision-on-2026-10-09).

Limit the main written request to three answer items:

1. Acceptance or rejection of the primary change and fallback, with reasons.
2. If the current rule is retained, an actual feasible path under the combined eligibility and financing conditions.
3. Change authority, responsible office, remaining steps, next decision/notice and applicable recruitment notice. An unknown date needs stated remaining steps and a next update plan.

Use supporting tables as attachments. Keep pre-entry accumulation separate from post-entry cash flow. A hypothetical loan is not an available product. Apply the ceiling at its actual screening stages. A seven-year saving comparison is a proposed benchmark, not every household's observed saving history. Follow the [income linkage review](income-linkage-review.md).

An upper saving-rate comparison should show the saved amount and the monthly amount left before household spending. Label 100-percent saving a theoretical bound with no living expenditure. Report a scenario that nearly reaches required own funds instead of choosing only deficits. Distinguish shortfalls from excesses, and retain financing and assessed-asset limits. These figures describe the strength of the saving assumption; they do not measure recipient wealth, a minimum living standard or support need by themselves.

## Review whether the programme reaches its intended households

When the user questions support for a household that can supply a large deposit, turn that concern into a programme-target question. Current income, accumulated housing wealth and required own funds are different quantities. Neither low current income nor ability to pay a deposit alone establishes support need. A conditional required cash amount is not an observed recipient asset holding or a selected wealth cutoff. Do not claim that a particular funding group dominates without recipient data.

Define the joint assessment: current income, held housing assets, initial own funds, continuing payment burden and the additional housing-stability effect. Ask the institution to evaluate the current criteria and selected changes on the same unit scope. Include effects on existing applicants, scarce-unit allocation and public cost. Request held contract/non-contract and financing aggregates separately from a new evaluation plan; do not assume the institution holds origin data or require a new individual audit.

Keep the selected priority and fallback. Connect deposit-linked asset adjustment to the relevant unit segment and to the same support-need and allocation review. Raising an asset ceiling can admit households with more assessed assets; acknowledge that policy choice instead of describing it as automatically targeted support. Do not silently replace the request with a new parental-wealth, funding-origin or minimum-income eligibility test.

A request to examine the combined criteria does not require the author to personally survey recipients. Use the notice and explicit scenarios to establish the question, and request relevant institutional records. Actual recipient shares or claims of preferential dominance require supporting data. Avoid making an author investigation disclaimer the lead of a structural proposal; preserve decision-relevant limits in the evidence scope.

When the user rejects excessive discounting of high-value locations, remove an unselected deep-discount remedy from the current memo and attachment. Ask for appropriate deposit/public-benefit design and eligibility aligned with that deposit. Do not turn this clarification into a ban on every discount, an immutable current price or an invented percentage. Keep financing feasibility, allocation and public cost review. See the [price-alignment clarification lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-the-price-alignment-clarification-on-2026-10-10).

For an authorised delegated review, freeze one candidate and give the same factual limits to separate constructed case-officer, legal/budget and team-lead roles. Record a parent council lens separately. Save each input hash and completed response. Reconcile objections, revise the body and have the same roles reread the changed input. Distinguish the scope of body review from the parent's calculation, attachment and delivery checks. A simulated reply cannot supply an actual policy decision, recipient distribution, institutional owner or acceptance probability.

Use product-specific loan conditions and a labelled rate scenario in the attachment. A larger loan reduces upfront own funds while increasing later payments. Keep policy and bank eligibility, deferral compatibility, assessed assets and approval unknowns beside the comparison. Describe an interest-only column as money before principal and other household costs. Check contract cash needed before loan execution; it is part of own funds, not a second amount to add. Align every duplicate rate field in the generator, calculation record, body and attachment after changing a scenario. Check current nested pointers after freezing the final rendered attachment.

See the [support-target revision lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-the-support-target-document-revision-on-2026-10-10) and [delegated review procedure](official-roleplay.md#run-a-delegated-document-rehearsal).

## Use one official case as the main record

Read existing filings, replies and the prepared packet before selecting a channel. Check current case status, receipt, deadline and the live final body before submitting. Record a phone explanation as an event; do not mark unanswered items complete because a call occurred.

The Seoul [general city suggestion/question route](https://eungdapso.seoul.go.kr/req/rectify/rectify.do) has an official result viewer. A proposal in substance does not automatically belong in a public idea-voting service. Preserve the actual prepared procedure unless a deliberate revision changes it. Reference the original case in a related improvement request instead of cloning the same text across several portals.

The [response portal guide](https://eungdapso.seoul.go.kr/not/answer/answer_center.do) describes additional-answer requests through satisfaction evaluation on the result screen. This does not establish that an in-progress case allows editing or appending its original body. Check the actual screen before promising that function. Use its observed due date; do not invent a universal response period or treat an internal target as a legal deadline.

After a reply, classify the three answer items as answered, partial or unanswered. Ask once for the remaining items in writing. For a missing policy position or implementation owner, consider the [official mayor-request route](https://eungdapso.seoul.go.kr/req/mayor_hope/mayor_hope.do), with prior receipts and the specific unresolved questions. Official routing does not establish direct mayor reading. A held-analysis disclosure request and a request to improve the rule serve different purposes; do not wait for disclosure before stating the selected change.

## Prepare a council route when a dated window matters

Use the [public reader research](reader-research.md) when choosing current committee members or adapting an introductory note. Verify the current council term, remit and professional-office responsibilities before using historical speeches. Separate a relevant public question from endorsement, actual reading or rule-changing authority. Keep the selected request and evidence consistent while changing the introduction and question order.

Verify a current official window, scope and responsible committee. Prepare the evidence while the agency case proceeds if waiting would miss the window. Give the elected body a focused institutional question: were the combined conditions evaluated, and what is the improvement decision? This is different from an individual qualification exception or a repeated copy of the same administrative request.

On 2026-10-09 the [Seoul council audit submission guide](https://www.smc.seoul.kr/board/BoardList.do?boardTypeId=162&menuId=001005009) states a 2026-09-04 to 2026-10-23 window. It accepts policy/project improvement matters and excludes private-interest matters. Some submissions may be reference material rather than audit questions. Identity is nonpublic, but content may be used publicly in the audit. Do not equate a nonpublic submission list with a promise that attachments will never be disclosed. The [committee guide](https://admin.smc.seoul.kr/publish/view.do?menuId=001004001) places the housing office and SH under the Housing and Space Committee. Recheck these dated facts before a later campaign.

Keep any earlier internal filing goal explicitly separate from the official cutoff. A date in a plan does not create a reminder, schedule or active monitor. Preserve notice provenance, calculation assumptions and substantive agency replies in the attachment set. Use the [sharing evidence procedure](sharing-evidence.md) for call summaries and identifying details.

Build a reviewable attachment before requesting any remaining transmission confirmation. A compact audit dossier should contain the selected change, the requested institutional decisions, matched recruitment criteria, reproducible savings and later-interest scenarios, a written-reply gap table and an attributed call summary. Preserve the original evidence privately. Remove applicant contacts and portal identifiers from a dossier that may be used publicly. Identity protection on the submission page does not protect identifying details inside an attachment. Keep an external archive copy labelled as such even after visually checking its tables; rendering is not an official-host comparison.

Separate the institutional submission from the private verification log. State the notice title, date, relevant pages and actual copy source in ordinary language. Preserve unresolved official-host retrieval and byte-comparison checks privately rather than repeating technical verification notes in the dossier. This does not elevate an archived copy to a verified official-host original. Keep assumptions and uncertainties that affect the requested decision beside the evidence. Label a participant's automated call account as a call-summary record, with a brief transcription/audio-check limit, rather than implying an audio-checked verbatim transcript. See the [reader-copy correction lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-council-reader-copy-correction-on-2026-10-09).

When the user requests independent reviews, divide persuasion, evidence/calculation and administrative answer design between read-only reviewers. Reconcile the findings before editing. Put the decisive comparison in the portal body with its nearby assumptions rather than relying entirely on an attachment. Ask separately for the primary change and fallback decisions. Request a path that satisfies the same notice's income, assets, household and financing conditions, plus the responsible office and next update when a decision date is unknown. A reasoned funding gap within one scenario supports a targeted review; it does not prove that every household is unable to enter or that the chosen policy change follows mathematically.

Lead that comparison with published payment conditions and savings under the stated benchmark. Derive the additional financing needed instead of centring an unsupported fixed loan amount. Ask for an actual eligible product, approval amount and execution timing that closes it. Preserve the difference between required financing and approved financing; do not infer that omission of an assumed loan proves no loan is available. Keep product-specific references separate from the main claim. When changing this presentation, remove stale loan-dependent gaps and combined-payment fields from current records while preserving the earlier model. Follow the [loan-clarity lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-removing-a-fixed-hypothetical-loan-on-2026-10-10).

Limit a paragraph about the institution's payment deferral to entry payment, periodic charge, rate changes and exit return. Put relevant bank-approval and assessment questions in the separate financing section. Keep internal calculation commentary in private verification records unless the reader needs it to interpret the displayed comparison. Removing an unused assumption does not require a new disclaimer about the number that was not calculated. Follow the [deferral-copy lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-focusing-the-deferral-paragraph-on-2026-10-10).

Keep known asset rules separate from unverified applications. Total assessed assets are not the same as cash available for a deposit. A notice may subtract specified recognised debts and use historical system-query values, while treatment of the actual bank loan, deferred deposit and screening stages remains unresolved. Verify the original page and page-number convention before adding an extract. A nominal deferred portion does not establish an additional cash requirement at exit. Read the settlement and actually paid deposit-return conditions, then model remaining interest, bank repayment and timing effects. Follow the [deferral-settlement correction](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-deferral-settlement-and-cashflow-clarification-on-2026-10-10).

Preserve the previous packet and PDF before a substantive revision. Finish layout review, inspect every final page, then align attachment hashes and freeze the revised envelope. If a generated candidate changes during visual QA, retain its previous envelope and record the corrected final hash. Update the current draft, campaign state and all nested execution-plan summaries together. Mark a previously entered body for replacement. An approval request for the older body and attachment count cannot approve the revised transmission. Keep personal eligibility expiry or transition suggestions separate from the selected institutional request. See the [review lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-independent-council-document-review-on-2026-10-09).

Inspect the actual writing route before assuming an existing city login works. The council writing route observed on 2026-10-09 required its own account or identity authentication. Its SNS login notice excluded audit submissions. Do not reuse another portal's saved credentials for a different destination. The user later specifically authorised council account creation with generated and locally stored initial credentials. Follow that current instruction and the [registration authentication procedure](remote-runbook.md#council-phone-authentication), including action-time agreement confirmation. Preserve the current authentication page for handoff and continue independent preparation. A completed local dossier is not a prepared live form or an accepted submission.

If the tracker does not support the elected body's portal and procedure, retain a separate private campaign execution record with the destination, exact body, attachment hashes, authentication state, attempted state and any later primary receipt. Do not assign a council tip to a national petition portal to satisfy validation. Extend the tracker contract and verify it before importing such records. The campaign record must clearly distinguish preparation, an attempt of unknown outcome and verified acceptance.

For a dated action calendar, give each row a target date, action, completion evidence and any prerequisite. Distinguish the official cutoff from the internal filing goal and an earlier backup day. Base overdue follow-up on the observed case deadline, not a date invented for the calendar. A received reply can trigger review immediately; an unanswered but still in-time case does not establish delay. Include an optional DM only after actual filing and separate sending authorisation. Keep rows planned until execution evidence exists. Preserve the reviewed plan and frozen packet; store the date expansion as a separate private artifact. Follow the [dated calendar lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-dated-action-planning-on-2026-10-09).

## Compare public-asset uses when the user adds that policy question

Separate the reason a public asset was acquired from the reason to retain its current use. A published acquisition standard does not establish the actual unit's acquisition price, ownership, sale authority or opportunity cost. Distinguish a tenant deposit, historical acquisition cost, current sale value and public subsidy. Keep a family-independent access benchmark as a programme evaluation question, not a new family-origin eligibility test or an asserted universal legal duty.

Compare retention, selected admission-rule changes, reinvestment within the responsible jurisdiction and conditional inter-jurisdiction cooperation. Do not assume a city can sell any public rental unit or buy replacements outside its jurisdiction. Request the relevant authority and constraints. Include location, social mixing, commute and caregiving access instead of treating a cheaper remote unit as equivalent by default.

Evaluate alternatives over the same horizon. Reconcile sale proceeds with deposit returns, related debts and transaction costs without double counting. Apply the same treatment to replacement-unit deposits and their future return obligations. Compare acquisition and operating costs, household payments, supported households and years, and remaining public assets. An unsupported sale price divided by an assumed replacement price does not establish the number of homes that can be provided. Cheap historical acquisition alone does not prove retention is the best present use.

When the user authorises a concrete revision, incorporate a bounded comparative review alongside the selected current-rule improvement. Keep the requested decisions explicit. A broader review must not silently replace or postpone the existing admission-rule proposal. Distinguish existing institutional records from a new review plan. Regroup the prepared answer items when needed, preserve the delivery and response status of accepted cases, and identify newly prepared questions as not yet submitted. Do not mark historical unanswered cases as having received the new question.

Follow the [public-asset comparison lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-public-asset-alternative-comparison-on-2026-10-10).

## Audit references and build conditional eligibility tables

Audit each active reference against the particular claim it supports. Opening a URL or recognising an institution is not verification of that claim. Keep the notice title, date, household size, floor area, page convention and provenance together. Record remaining original-file equivalence checks privately. A checked archived copy does not become an official-host original. Preserve separate authority, calculation and interpretation limits instead of saying that every reference is verified.

Use the recruitment notice's planned entry year when presenting carrying costs. A valid current-year interest calculation may not be the rate at a later entry date. Identify a published borrowing ceiling as a conditional example rather than an approved loan or the household's minimum own cash. Reconcile product, collateral and application-channel differences before combining public limits.

For salary-by-assets tables, state whether salary is gross household pay or net pay. A monthly assessed-income ceiling multiplied by twelve is a comparison equivalent; it is not a universal gross-pay or net-pay eligibility rule. Define assessed net assets separately from cash available for payment. If a simple table assumes both are equal, state the liquid-assets and zero-recognised-debt assumptions next to it. Do not add past savings again to assets already held.

Separate the income/asset screening result, the remaining payment need, financing approval and eventual selection. When family support is unspecified, derive the required amount and state whether the scenario is a gift, loan or guarantee. Evaluate assets before and after a gift. Historical query timing alone does not establish that a later gift is exempt. Check continuous qualification requirements through entry. Do not subtract ordinary family loans as recognised institutional debt; verify any stated exception independently.

Keep the same axes and rules in the with-support and without-support tables. Show unresolved cells explicitly rather than inventing a green admission result. A fully assessed cash-only scenario can expose a gap between an asset ceiling and required payment, but does not prove that every applicant is unable to enter or that a particular family type receives most benefits. Ask the agency for an actual eligible funding and assessment path. Keep the user's internal audit separate from the material intended for external transmission.

## Treat a direct message as supplemental routing

Verify social accounts through an official institution homepage. Do not rely on an aggregator or a similar account name. The [Seoul mayor homepage](https://mayor.seoul.go.kr/index.do), checked 2026-10-09, directly links [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ohsehoon4u/) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ohsehoon4you). This establishes linked account provenance, not DM receiving settings, direct mayor reading or response probability.

After an actual official filing, a short optional DM can state the issue, selected change, receipt and department-routing request. Send only when the user authorises sending. A request to assess DM effectiveness or make a plan does not itself authorise delivery. Do not make a draft say an unfiled proposal was submitted. Avoid full personal conversations, staff phone numbers and unrelated finances. Consider a further message only when substantive new material exists. Unanswered DMs do not justify repeated messages or replace official follow-up. Record any response as a separate source.

## Define completion and preserve decisions

The first milestone is an itemised written position with reasons, owner and next step. A receipt, phone call, read marker or generic review statement does not meet that milestone. Policy success requires a changed rule and actual recruitment notice. A reasoned refusal supplies a decision for the next proposal; it is not an adopted improvement.

Do not infer that separate legal rules explain away their combined effect. Ask for the exact change authority and required consultation. Current departmental press material can resolve a stale routing label, but it does not prove unilateral power to change an income ceiling. Preserve relevant countercontext, such as improvement work already described by the agency. An automated call transcript without audio comparison supports attributed paraphrase, not a finding of deliberate concealment, universal document absence or illegality.

Save the operation plan, official memo, optional DM, council candidate, source map and reviewer conclusions privately. Hash the frozen packet, policy focus and ledger before and after planning. A condensed memo is a revision candidate until adopted through the [packet revision procedure](computer-use.md). Commit only the reusable method and deidentified handoff. Follow the [dated campaign lesson](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-official-campaign-planning-on-2026-10-09).
