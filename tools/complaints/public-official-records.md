# Research public officials and declared asset changes

Use this procedure when the user requests a public official's career, past conduct, policy positions or published asset history. Extend the [reader map](reader-research.md), but keep the selected complaint and its delivery status unchanged. Save named research and raw evidence in private complaint storage. Publish only the reusable method and deidentified handoff.

## Divide the research and preserve the input

When the user requests subagents, divide named officials and asset disclosures into separate tasks. Assign disjoint evidence directories. One parent reconciles the sources and owns public documentation and Git integration. Do not describe multiple research agents as independent reviewers of the complaint.

Preserve the current proposal, attachment, policy focus and ledger hashes. Record the research date, current office and exact identity. Check the official institution and dated career page before attaching a historical statement or disclosure to the person. A name match alone does not resolve a namesake or retain an old position.

## Record careers and public positions

Use official biographies, original minutes, bill texts, voting records and published interviews. Search summaries, media descriptions and member-page headlines are discovery leads. Read the relevant original passage and its countercontext before assigning a policy emphasis.

| Record | Minimum fields | Interpretation limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Career | Person, institution or role, period, official source, observed date | A biography is the institution's published account; it does not establish private motives |
| Party affiliation | Official current affiliation and historical date when relevant | Party membership does not establish the person's position on the requested amendment |
| Public statement | Speaker, date, programme, source location and surrounding exchange | Preserve reservations, alternative remedies and different programme populations |
| Bill activity | Bill identity, exact text, proposer role, submission date and current stage | Lead sponsorship, co-signing, committee action and an individual recorded vote are different events |
| Individual vote | Named voting record, proposal/version, meeting date and vote | A sponsor list or committee passage is not a recorded individual vote |
| Public ideology | Express self-description or a clearly attributed published position | Do not infer private beliefs from wealth, family circumstances or an unsupported label |
| Conduct or controversy | Original decision, ruling or disciplinary record, date, procedural status and response | A criticism or allegation does not establish wrongdoing or a final judgment |

Describe observed policy priorities by topic and time. Examples include allocation to vulnerable households, policy effectiveness, family-care burdens, financing feasibility and administrative follow-through. A repeated concern supports a dated interpretation of that topic. It does not support a total personality assessment, acceptance probability or a claim that the official endorses the selected remedy.

Preserve favourable and adverse context. A relevant housing question may point toward lower deposits or different financing instead of income-ceiling abolition. The user's current demands remain the comparison baseline.

## Compare asset disclosures on the same basis

Start from the [PETI public search](https://www.peti.go.kr/peOptpListVie.do) and the original disclosure. Read the [publication explanation](https://www.peti.go.kr/prptOptp.do) and [permitted nondisclosure explanation](https://www.peti.go.kr/nticDen.do) before comparing totals. On 2026-10-09, PETI states that its integrated search supplies disclosures from 2022-08-01. Earlier records need another original archive.

The official explanation places metropolitan council members under the Government Public Service Ethics Committee. A city's own disclosure may instead cover district councillors or other officials. Do not assume the city gazette alone contains a metropolitan councillor's declaration.

For each disclosure, record:

1. Original URL or official disclosure identifier, file hash and relevant pages.
2. Publication date, actual valuation/reference date and filing type: regular, initial, re-registration, retirement or correction.
3. Original currency unit, reported total, prior total and reported change.
4. Declaration scope, including the declarant and any included spouse or direct relatives. Note permitted nondisclosure and scope changes without copying relatives' identities.
5. The disclosed reasons for material changes. Keep valuation changes, transactions, savings, debts, inheritance or gifts as reported categories, not independently proven cash flows.
6. Comparability and the remaining source or scope gap.

Preserve the original unit before converting thousands of won into won or hundred-million won. Reconcile previous amount plus change with current amount using the correct debt signs. Check the first and final totals on the original pages. For a PDF table, read the complete relevant pages, render the decisive pages and confirm the columns visually. OCR without image verification remains pending.

Use a comparable series to calculate a change. Mark a newly elected official's first filing as a starting point when an earlier comparable disclosure is unavailable. A missing year is unknown, not zero. Candidate-election declarations and ethics declarations may share a reference date but have different forms or corrections; retain both bases until reconciled.

Compare the current total from one filing with the prior total stated in the next filing. If they differ, preserve both values and the exact gap. The next filing's reported change and a subtraction of two independently published current totals are different quantities. Do not silently replace either value or invent a correction filing. A discrepancy is a source-reconciliation question, not evidence of a reporting violation.

Declared household wealth is not the official's personal liquid assets or annual income. Permitted nondisclosure can exclude some relatives' assets. An increase in a reported total does not alone prove a corresponding income increase, a policy interest conflict or illicit accumulation. A conflict-of-interest allegation requires the separate public-interest and legal evidence appropriate to that allegation.

If calculating a declarant-only net amount, select the original rows whose relation is the declarant, exclude category subtotals and family totals, and subtract that person's liability rows. Retain only those selected row locators as calculation provenance. Reconcile both the arithmetic and the cited row scope; a correct amount can still have incorrect provenance. Label the result as a derived calculation, not the official household total.

Exclude individual home addresses, account numbers, identity numbers, private contacts and relatives' names from the user-facing comparison. Retain only the disclosure scope and aggregate values needed to interpret the official record. Do not use wealth to infer ideology or to construct pressure messages.

## Reconcile and apply the findings

The parent reads the agents' actual sources, checks material dates and amounts, and resolves contradictions before writing the combined report. Retain source, observation, confirmed assertion and policy interpretation as separate layers. Record retrieval failures, incomplete extractions and unverified claims without treating them as institutional absence.

Use the findings to sharpen institutional review questions and source references. Keep identical demands and assumptions for all readers. A biography or an asset table does not change a proposal or authorise contact. Named research contexts can supply questions for a constructed [official review role](official-roleplay.md); invented statements must not be attributed to a real person.

Before completion, check source gates, local links, unit conversions, privacy exclusions, preserved input hashes and repository doctors. Document the practical lessons in [the complaint lessons](lessons-learned.md). Integrate only intended public files through the existing managed complaint worktree and release all agent locks.

See the [dated career and asset lessons](lessons-learned.md#lessons-from-public-careers-and-asset-disclosures-on-2026-10-09) for examples of namesake exclusion, filing-basis changes, date conflicts and cross-filing total gaps.
