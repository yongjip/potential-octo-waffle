## Decisions and limits

The user explicitly requested subagents. Two agents split the officials' career and policy records, and a third examined published asset disclosures. The parent reconciled their evidence. This is delegated research, not independent reviews of the complaint or a census of all past conduct, votes, disciplinary decisions or judgments.

Distinguish current offices from historical roles, and retain a leadership start-date conflict between an official biography and actual appointment minutes. Original bill submission, sponsorship, committee alternatives, individual recorded votes, promulgation and commencement are separate events. Official releases and interviews are attributed public statements; they do not establish every allegation or a private worldview.

A full original transcript contained an especially direct description of the affected household. It also described other financing remedies and reservations. Related issue recognition does not establish endorsement of the selected income or asset change. One researcher reused the other researcher's original snapshot with its actual observation time and hashes, rather than representing the copy as a new retrieval.

Asset evidence covers three regular disclosure years and five officials. Publication dates differ from year-end valuation dates. Some prior filings belong to district councils. Permitted nondisclosure, included relatives, debt and unlisted-share valuation can change comparability. One consecutive series has an unresolved prior-total gap. Retain both figures and distinguish a filing's stated change from subtraction across published current totals.

The parent caught a provenance defect: derived declarant-only amounts had correct arithmetic but locators included other family rows. The researcher corrected the locators and revalidated fifteen records. Both numerical and source-row scope checks are required. Named comparisons omit relatives' identities, addresses, accounts and private contacts. Wealth is not evidence of ideology, motives or wrongdoing.

Detailed research and original disclosures remain private. Public integration contains only the reusable procedure and this deidentified handoff. The selected complaint, attachment, policy focus and actual filing ledger were preserved. No external message, filing, account action, schedule or roleplay was performed in this task.
